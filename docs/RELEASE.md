# Native release checks

The refactor has not been tested sending a real message on a physical iPhone. JavaScript checks, browser tests, and generated native configuration do not prove hardware behavior. Complete these checks on signed iOS and Android builds before release.

- Start fresh with no backend/environment credentials. Complete or skip each onboarding step. Manual checks work without camera, contacts, notifications, or location permissions.
- Save/edit/remove/default a person; restart and verify SQLite persistence. Choose a contact without broad iOS access, test Android grant/denial and contacts with multiple/no numbers. Validate international formatting and country-code correction.
- Create QR only after consent; revoke consent and verify it disappears. Scan valid, malformed, wrong-version, oversized, and unrelated QR codes. Cancel review without saving, save after review, select default, and reject duplicates. Confirm the QR is readable across devices.
- Share an invitation and contact details separately. Inspect the actual shared text: no phone number in the URL, no pairing claims, and accurate installed-app versus install-page behavior. Verify links with the app foregrounded, closed, and the phone locked.
- Pick Gym, edit its message, select a person and tap Send. Confirm the native composer receives the normalized phone and text. Rapid taps open one composer. Test unsupported messaging, cancel, send, scheduled send where supported, and unknown. Cancellation must not appear as sent; Android must display unverified. Editing recipient/text in the composer must not cause history to claim it knows the final content or delivery.
- Verify a confirmed send triggers only the optional local sender confirmation and cancels pending departure reminders. Disable the confirmation toggle and notifications permission. Unknown/cancelled results must never show a success notification. Test storage failure and app termination during composition without encouraging a duplicate send.
- Verify native history across restarts; launch/foreground/History/new-entry cleanup after 72 hours (including time-zone/DST change). Home should show only the most recent confirmed action in retention. Clear history; clear all local data with confirmation; verify saved places stop monitoring and pending notifications are cancelled.
- Field-test enabled/disabled locations, geofence duplicates, cooldown, brief exit, return, drift, overlap, poor accuracy, permission revocation, force quit and reboot. Every notification opens an editable check-in; no background sending occurs. Observe OS delays and battery use over several days.
- Departure reminders: 1/5/10-minute delays, return cancellation, confirmed-send cancellation, disabling/deleting a place, changing delay, pause/resume, and clear data. No partner/together/proximity claim is supported.
- Add each supported iOS widget family manually. Verify cold/warm/locked-phone opening to Home, all saved shortcuts accessible, and no automatic composer launch or sending. Android uses in-app shortcuts/notifications.
- Test anonymous RevenueCat purchase, cancellation, pending purchase, restore, expiration, refund, and store management in sandbox. Confirm actual localized monthly price, publisher privacy/terms, and restore limitations. Billing must not block local core use.
- Check VoiceOver/TalkBack, larger text, keyboard dismissal, small phones, contrast/dark mode, offline editing, and permission-denial recovery.

Choose final app IDs and signing, verify generated widget entitlements, camera/contact/location usage strings and Android blocked permissions. Publish support/privacy/terms/install pages, complete store disclosures for local data, messaging handoff, optional background location, and RevenueCat purchase data. Generate store screenshots and provide review instructions requiring no login. No store listing, signing, submission, or physical-device validation is implied by this repository.
